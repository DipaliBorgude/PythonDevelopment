About Us

Kohinoor Bank is a trusted financial institution committed to delivering secure, innovative, and customer-centric banking solutions. Inspired by the brilliance and strength of the Kohinoor diamond, our bank stands as a symbol of reliability, transparency, and excellence in the financial world. Founded with a vision to empower individuals and businesses, Kohinoor Bank offers a wide range of services including savings accounts, loans, digital banking, and investment solutions. We strive to make banking simple, accessible, and efficient for everyone, whether in urban cities or rural communities. At Kohinoor Bank, we believe that banking is not just about transactions — it’s about building lasting relationships. Our dedicated team works tirelessly to understand customer needs and provide personalized financial guidance to help achieve their goals. With cutting-edge technology and a strong focus on security, we ensure that your money is always safe and your banking experience is seamless. Our digital platforms enable customers to manage their finances anytime, anywhere with ease. Kohinoor Bank is not just a bank — it is a partner in your financial journey, helping you grow, succeed, and shine.
